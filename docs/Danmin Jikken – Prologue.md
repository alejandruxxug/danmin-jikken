### 📼 Orientation reel

> Day look from here: line art, CRT, the screen glass. Driftwell's mascot on each card, one cheerful voice-over line under it. ~30 s. **Any key skips a card**; on a second run the whole reel can be skipped. It teaches the desk rules from [[Danmin Jikken – Game Design#📜 What each stamp means]].

[CARD 1: the mascot waves in front of the Halcyon Rest doors]

**Orientation reel**: Welcome to your first day at Halcyon Rest! A rested Shoal is a happy Shoal!

[CARD 2: a Settler ID and a referral side by side, check marks popping in]

**Orientation reel**: Every guest brings papers! Their **name** must match their **Settler ID**. The ID must be **in date**. The referral needs **Dr. Barrow's stamp** and a **sleep diagnosis**.

[CARD 3: three stamps bounce in: ADMIT · REFER · DISCHARGE]

**Orientation reel**: Papers in order? **ADMIT!** Papers wrong? **REFER** them home to their clinic. A guest back with a wristband and no new episodes? **DISCHARGE!**

[CARD 4: a patient nods off in a chair, a 💤 bubble; a hand clicks them, a nurse hurries in]

**Orientation reel**: Guests may drift off. That's normal! **Click a sleeping guest** and our staff will tuck them in. Never let a guest sleep on the floor.

[CARD 5: the mascot, smiling a little too long]

**Orientation reel**: Your rules for the day are pinned to your desk. Follow them, and you'll rest easy too!

[MUSIC: Driftwell jingle, last note held a beat too long]

→ ☀️ **Day 1** starts (the desk). The memo reads: *"Admit only patients with a valid referral."*
