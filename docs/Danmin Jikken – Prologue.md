[BG: black]
[SFX: squeaking wheels, slow]

*Someone is pushing me.*

**Mayu** *(smile)*: There you are. Don't be scared, okay? You're in good hands.

**Hana** *(soft)*: Ten.

[FX: the text box types by itself, slower and slower]

*Ten… nine… eight…*

*seven…*

[FX: eyes close. The eyelids come in from the top and bottom of the screen.]
