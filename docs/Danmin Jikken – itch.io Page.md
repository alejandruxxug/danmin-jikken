## Main description

**Halcyon Rest Sanatorium** is a bright, cheerful seaside clinic on the ruined coast, owned by the smiling **Driftwell Corporation**. For months, people from the Shoals have been arriving *sleepy*. The official diagnosis is narcolepsy. Inland folk just shrug: *"Shoal folk are always half-asleep anyway."*

You're the new **admissions clerk**.

**☀️ By day,** you work the reception desk. Patients walk up to your counter with their papers: a Settler ID, a referral, a sleep log. Check them against the day's rules, then stamp **ADMIT** or **DISCHARGE**. Pan across the waiting room, flip up the Driftwell terminal, wake the patients who drop off mid-sentence, and find a bed for everyone before the shift ends. Try not to look too closely at the sleep logs. They all say the same time.

**🌙 By night,** the white lights go out and the clinic turns grey. The night nurses are still here. **Mayu** is kind, much too kind, and she hasn't slept in nineteen days. **Hana** is quiet, and she keeps leaving you notes. Neither of them will tell you why they refuse to sleep.

Three days, three nights. Who you admit, what you read and who you trust all change how it ends.

*The prejudice is the cover-up.*

---

## Features
- **A day shift you actually play:** a FNAF 2-style reception desk crossed with Papers, Please. Check papers, stamp them, manage beds and keep an eye on sleeping patients.
- **A night shift you read:** a short, branching psychological-horror visual novel.
- **3 routes and 4 endings,** one of them secret.
- **Hand-drawn pixel art** at 480×270 with boiling linework, integer scaling and an optional CRT filter.
- **About 20 minutes** per playthrough, so it's built to be replayed.
- **High scores** saved locally, and the hospital remembers your previous runs.
- Native builds for **Windows, macOS and Linux**.

## ⚠️ Content warning
> This game contains depictions of **suicide, self-harm, death and graphic gore**, and **strong flashing lights** (a strobe effect). A warning is shown at launch, and **Settings → Reduce flashing** turns the strobe down.
>
> If you're struggling, please reach out to someone. In the US you can call or text **988**. Elsewhere, see **findahelpline.com**.

## Controls

| Input | Action |
|---|---|
| Mouse | Pan the desk, pick up papers, stamp, click sleepers, choose dialogue |
| **F11** / Alt(Option)+Enter / Cmd+F | Fullscreen |
| **F10** | CRT filter on/off |
| **Esc** | Skip the prologue (after your first run) and the tutorial hints |

Settings: CRT filter, reduce flashing, auto-play, tutorial hints, music and SFX volume.

## Install
Download the zip for your system, unzip it and run **danmin**. Everything is built in, with nothing extra to install. Your settings and scores are saved next to the executable.
*(macOS: if it says the app is from an unidentified developer, right-click → Open.)*
