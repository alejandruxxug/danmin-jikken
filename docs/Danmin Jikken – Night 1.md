# 🌙 Danmin Jikken – Night 1

Back to [[Danmin Jikken – Script]] · Before: [[Danmin Jikken – Prologue]] + ☀️ Day 1 · Next: ☀️ Day 2, then [[Danmin Jikken – Night 2]]

> **Goal: "go home."** You wake in bed 4 of Ward C and think it's closing time at the desk. ~420 words. Choice 1 picks who you lean toward.
> **Day 1 before this:** Old Cecily made you laugh, then fell asleep mid-joke. Matty was admitted.

---

## Waking up

[FX: white fades to grey, slowly, like eyes adjusting]
[BG: ward C, dark. A row of beds. You're sitting up in one.]
[SFX: a monitor beeping, far away]

*Closing time already.*

*I must have dozed off at the desk.*

*My uniform feels thin tonight.*

[BG: corridor → nurses' station]

**Mayu** *(bright)*: Oh! Look who's up!

**Mayu** *(smile)*: Off home already? You always say that.

*Always? It's my first day.*

**Mayu** *(smile)*: Here. Coffee. It's terrible, but it's warm.

*She holds the cup with both hands when she gives it to me. Her fingers are shaking.*

**Mayu** *(laugh)*: Don't look at my hands! Long shift. Long… lots of shifts.

[FX: on the counter, next to the coffee pot: a pale can with a thin black logo, and a syringe]

*Next to the coffee pot there's a pale can.* PALE MSTR. *And a syringe.*

**Mayu** *(smile)*: Oh, that? Nurse stuff! Coffee stopped working weeks ago.

**Mayu** *(soft)*: You know, it's nice. Having someone new. Someone who stays.

**Mayu** *(blank)*: If you ever left, I'd…

**Mayu** *(laugh)*: Ha! I'd be so *bored*. Kidding. You should see your face.

[SFX: footsteps]

*Someone walks past behind her. Another nurse. She doesn't look at us.*

*She puts a folded paper on the counter, right in front of me, and keeps walking.*

**Mayu** *(pout)*: That's Hana. Don't mind her. She doesn't like new people.

## Choice 1

> - **Stay and talk to Mayu** → [[#1A Stay with Mayu]] `→ set liked_mayu`
> - **Go read Hana's note** → [[#1B Read Hana's note]] `→ set liked_hana`

### 1A Stay with Mayu

`→ set liked_mayu`

*I leave the note where it is.*

**Mayu** *(happy)*: You're staying? Okay. Okay! Sit, sit.

**Mayu** *(smile)*: Do you want to know a secret? I haven't slept in nineteen days.

**Mayu** *(laugh)*: Kidding! …Mostly.

**Mayu** *(soft)*: Nobody here sleeps. It's kind of our thing.

*She laughs again. A beat too long.*

→ [[#The monitor]]

### 1B Read Hana's note

`→ set liked_hana`

**Mayu** *(blank)*: Oh. Sure.

*I unfold it.*

**Note** *(Hana's handwriting, small and neat)*: Your stamp on bed 4 was wrong.

*Bed 4? I didn't stamp a bed 4.*

*I look up. Hana is at the end of the corridor, watching me read it.*

**Hana** *(flat)*: Go back to bed, Four.

*Then she's gone.*

**Mayu** *(smile)*: See? Not a people person.

→ [[#The monitor]]

## The monitor

[BG: nurses' station, the ward monitor behind the counter]
[SFX: beep… beep… beep…]

*Behind the counter there's a screen. Green lines, one for every patient. Rising and falling.*

*Matty's name is on one of them.*

[SFX: the beep becomes one long tone]
[FX: Matty's line goes flat]

*Flat.*

*One second. Two. Three.*

[SFX: beep.]
[FX: the line comes back]

**Mayu** *(smile)*: Oh, that happens. They dream hard in here.

*I count the lines. Six patients on the board.*

*Seven lines.*

[FX: **first red pixel** of the game: the seventh line blinks red, once]

*The seventh one has no name.*

## Back to sleep

**Mayu** *(soft)*: Hey. You look so tired.

*I want to say I'm going home. I stand up, and the floor tilts.*

**Mayu** *(smile)*: Shh. Sleep tight.

[SFX: a small click]
*A pinch in my arm.*

[FX: eyes close]
[FX: hard cut to WHITE]
